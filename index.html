01 · the brief

reinventing the jewellery counter.

Independent jewellers either lock lakhs in loose stones or lose customers when the exact ring isn't in stock. At the Atelier removes that trade-off: a real-time 3D configurator at the counter plus a tactile Prototype Kit means zero inventory risk and Mumbai master fulfilment.

  • Make jewellers feel the shift: sell what the customer imagines, not what's in the showcase.
  • Hook in 3 seconds: macro diamond refraction + the blue linen box unboxing.
  • 3-step proof: sit down together → design live in 3D → close with 12-day delivery.
