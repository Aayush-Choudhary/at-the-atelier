01 · the brief

reinventing the jewellery counter.

Independent jewellers either lock lakhs in loose stones or lose customers when the exact ring isn't in stock. At the Atelier removes that trade-off: a real-time 3D configurator at the counter plus a tactile Prototype Kit means zero inventory risk and Mumbai master fulfilment.

  • Make jewellers feel the shift: sell what the customer imagines, not what's in the showcase.
  • Hook in 3 seconds: macro diamond refraction + the blue linen box unboxing.
  • 3-step proof: sit down together → design live in 3D → close with 12-day delivery.

03 · why we recommend option 02 (cgi + mg)

maximum luxury, lower cost.

  • Client Rough Script Ready (Saves Pre-Prod Cost): We skip scratch copywriting and move straight into visual direction, storyboard & camera animatic.
  • Client 3D Models Provided (Saves Modeling Cost): By ingesting your existing ring & kit CAD meshes, we bypass scratch 3D modeling and focus budget on lookdev & animation.
  • No Photoshoot Quality Bottlenecks: Macro jewelry live shoots suffer from harsh reflections, shallow focus, and rigid framing. CGI guarantees flawless diamond fire & caustics.
  • Infinite Creative Transitions: Effortlessly transition from the physical sapphire velvet tray straight into the floating 3D SaaS configurator UI.
the smart middle ground
