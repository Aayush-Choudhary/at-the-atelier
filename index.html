01 · the brief

reinventing the jewellery counter.

Independent jewellers face an impossible dilemma: lock hundreds of thousands in capital holding loose stones in display cases, or watch customers walk away when the exact ring isn't in stock.

At the Atelier dissolves this boundary. With a real-time 3D configurator SaaS at the counter and a tactile luxury Prototype Kit, jewellers sell any bespoke ring with zero inventory risk and Mumbai master fulfillment.

  • Make jewellers feel the shift: Sell what the customer imagines, not what's trapped in the showcase.
  • Hook in 3 seconds: Mesmerizing macro diamond refraction + tactile unboxing of the luxury blue linen box.
  • Clear 3-step proof: Sit down together → Design live in 3D → Close the order with 12-day delivery.

04 · rough 1-minute narrative flow

how the film plays.

0–8s the hook

Showcase reflection. A customer hesitates over static rings in the glass case. "Why settle for what's already made?"

8–22s the kit

Gloved hands lift the royal blue Atelier linen lid. Modular trays reveal 75+ heads and 77+ shanks. Tactile click.

22–48s 3d configurator

Seamless transition to screen: real-time 3D ring rotates. Stone shape transforms live (Round → Oval → Emerald). Metal switches to yellow gold. Floating UI cards confirm specs.

48–60s the reveal & cta

The finished ring locks in. "No inventory. Mumbai master crafting in 12 days." Brand reveal: At the Atelier. "Your store. Your name. Your atelier."

A precision-timed narrative engineered to captivate both high-end jewellers and engaged couples within the first 3 seconds.
